Screen geometry

α is measured between the incident direction of travel and the rightward grating surface: 0° is grazing from the left, 90° is normal incidence, and 180° is grazing from the right. β runs from the rightward grating surface to the screen normal in the upper half-plane. At β = 90°, the screen is parallel to the grating.

d₀ is the perpendicular distance from the incidence point to the screen. Its perpendicular foot is x = 0. Positive x points left when β = 90°. The diagram shows a schematic segment of an ideal infinite screen.

sin θₘ = cos α − mλΛ · xₘ = d₀ cos(θₘ + β) / sin(θₘ + β)

Λ is groove density in m⁻¹, with grating spacing d = 1/Λ (in metres); use λ in metres in this equation. θ is measured from the grating normal. The plot is centered on zero. Hit colors match the rays; mark heights have no physical meaning. Labels give each order’s x coordinate, using the central wavelength for bandwidth or 550 nm for white light.
